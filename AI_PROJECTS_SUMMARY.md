## 🎯 Project Portfolio

### Tier 1: Production-Grade AI Systems

#### **1. GDG Event: AI-Enabled Customer Support Agent**

**Overview:**  
An intelligent, multi-tiered complaint resolution system that automates customer support operations while maintaining high service quality standards.

**Architecture:**

```
Guest Submission
    ↓
Identity Verification (AI)
    ↓
Complaint Categorization (ML Classification)
    ↓
Tier Assignment (0, 1, 2, or 3)
    ↓
Resolution Proposal (Intelligent Matching)
    ↓
Guest Selection & Approval
    ↓
Case Finalization & Closure
```

**AI Components:**
- Natural Language Processing for complaint understanding
- Classification algorithm for multi-tier categorization
- Rule-based resolution engine
- Identity verification system
- Case management automation

**Business Impact:**
- Faster complaint resolution
- Reduced manual workload
- Fair, tier-based compensation
- Improved guest satisfaction

**Scalability:**
- Designed for multi-industry deployment
- Extensible policy framework
- Customizable resolution tiers
- API-ready for enterprise integration

**Status:** Core development complete, ready for deployment  
**Next Steps:** Industry-specific customization, integration testing

**Key Features:**

**Enterprise Applications:**

**Technical Specifications:**
